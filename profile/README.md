# AGOberfeld

This is the from the work group of Prof. Dr. Daniel Oberfeld-Twistel, Johannes Gutenberg-Universität Mainz, Germany.
We do research on perception and cognition.

## Web page
https://www.staff.uni-mainz.de/oberfeld/index.html

## Research topics
 
- Audio-visual perception in traffic
  - Arrival-time estimation (time-to-contact estimation) and pedestrian crossing behavior based on auditory and visual information
  - Vehicle-sound related differences in pedestrian interactions with electric and conventional vehicles
  - Virtual acoustics and visual VR simulations
  - Road safety and human factors
- Psychoacoustics
- Psychological effects of color
